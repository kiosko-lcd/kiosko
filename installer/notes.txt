Kiosko is installed.

Open it from the Start menu ("Kiosko"), or go to http://127.0.0.1:7420/ in your
browser. If you chose "Start Kiosko when I sign in", it is already running in
the background; otherwise the Start menu entry starts it.

  Your settings:      %APPDATA%\Kiosko\config.json   (kept if you uninstall)
  The log:            %APPDATA%\Kiosko\kiosko.log
  Command line:       kiosko doctor   |   kiosko usb   |   kiosko service uninstall

USB panels (Thermalright Trofeo Vision and other LY panels)
Kiosko talks to them itself, but Windows needs the WinUSB driver on the panel,
installed once with Zadig (https://zadig.akeo.ie): pick the panel, choose
WinUSB, "Replace Driver". After that "kiosko usb" lists it, and in the editor,
Screens > Add screen > Detect panels offers it. While WinUSB is on the panel
the official Thermalright software will not see it; restore the original driver
from Device Manager if you need it back.
Details: https://github.com/kiosko-lcd/kiosko/blob/main/docs/usb.md
